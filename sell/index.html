Good to know when selling in Hawaii

A few things that are different here. We'll walk you through each one.

7.25%of the sale price

HARPTA withholding

If you live outside Hawaii, this is generally held back at closing as a prepayment of state tax. You can apply to reduce it, and any overpayment comes back when you file.

0.10%and up

Conveyance tax

Paid by the seller at closing. The rate starts at 10 cents per $100 and rises with the sale price, and is higher for homes that aren't owner-occupied.

Fee simpleLeasehold

How your land is held

Fee simple means you own the land too. Leasehold means the land is leased. It changes your buyer pool and price, so we plan around it.

Seller disclosure

Hawaii sellers must disclose known material facts about the home. We help you complete it carefully, so there are no surprises in escrow.

General information, not tax or legal advice. Talk with a tax professional about your situation.
