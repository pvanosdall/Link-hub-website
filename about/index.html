My story

I'm a Realtor with Hawaii LUX Homes of eXp Realty. I live in Kailua and work with buyers and sellers all over Oahu, whether you're buying your first home, relocating from the mainland, moving on military orders, or getting ready to sell.

I'm also a landscape photographer. When I'm not working, you'll usually find me out with a camera on the Windward side or up on a ridge trail. That habit of noticing light, space, and detail shapes how I look at homes, and how I help them stand out.

On the marketing side, I write a monthly newsletter and articles about Oahu neighborhoods and the market, so my clients always know what's happening. My approach is simple: no pressure, just clear information, so you can make a confident decision.
